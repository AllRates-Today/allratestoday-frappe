# Changelog

## 0.1.0 - 2026-11-01

- First release.
- AllRatesToday Settings: rate source (RBI/FBIL reference, CBIC customs, Indian bank TT card, ECB reference, live market rates, any other central bank code), base currency, currency list, buying/selling split, inverse pairs.
- Daily scheduled sync into ERPNext Currency Exchange; hourly option for live market rates.
- "Sync now" and "Apply ERPNext provider preset" buttons.
- Keyless sources need no account.
